# 06 — Structured branch

## Claim status: OPEN

No theorem is known here showing that a generalized arithmetic progression
with suitable 2-adic alignment satisfies LRC, has positive Fourier
certificate, or reduces to a smaller LRC instance. Any induction would also
need to prove preservation of the threshold \(1/(N+1)\), integrality,
distinctness, and the exact rescaling.

Accordingly, the structured implication requested by the research program
is recorded as an **OPEN LEMMA**, not as a proof step.
