# TASK 14 — The Step-Parameter Clearance Word

## Starting point

TASK 13 derived exact clearances at \(W\pm h b_*\), completely classified the first boundary \(h=1\), and proved that the direct endpoint Fourier expansion is geometric rather than Kloosterman. Mixed residue populations \(1\) and \(q-1\) block the two first boundaries, but \((1,3,4)\) is safe at \(h=3\).

## Objective

Determine whether the finite clearance word in \(h\) forces a common safe point, and identify precisely when its modular structure could support nontrivial exponential-sum estimates.

## Required work

1. Express each condition \(c_i^\pm(h)>0\) as an exact union of integer intervals or residue classes in \(h\).
2. Determine a sufficient union/intersection theorem for \(1\le h<q\), testing it exhaustively before claiming it.
3. Preserve the runner labels and distinguish the \(+1\), \(-1\), and central residue populations.
4. Find the smallest configuration for which no \(W\pm h b_*\), \(1\le h<q\), is safe; do not call it an LRC counterexample.
5. If the initial range fails, prove the least natural enlarged range justified by periodicity rather than selecting one experimentally.
6. Only invoke incomplete-sum estimates after deriving an explicit sum and bounding its completion coefficients. Record whether the phase is linear, inverse, or genuinely mixed.
7. Prove a new infinite mixed-fringe family, or state the exact simultaneous-avoidance obstruction.
8. Add reproducible exact code, deterministic results, proofs, claim statuses, and the next narrow task.
