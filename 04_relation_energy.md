# 04 — Relation energy

## Claim status: OPEN

For a finite configuration \(V\), define the ordinary \(k\)-fold additive
energy
\[
E_k(V)=\#\{(x_1,\ldots,x_k,y_1,\ldots,y_k)\in V^{2k}:
\sum_jx_j=\sum_jy_j\}.
\]
The signed dual quantity from `02_poisson_certificate.md` is a weighted
sum over coefficient relations, not directly a count of equal-sum tuples.
Therefore no implication between odd Fourier mass and \(E_k(V)\) is asserted.

The required missing theorem is an explicit, hypothesis-checked implication
of the form
\[
M_{\rm odd}\ge\eta\Longrightarrow E_k(V)\ge f(\eta,N),
\]
possibly for a weighted or higher-order energy better matched to the
coefficients. This remains an **OPEN LEMMA**.
