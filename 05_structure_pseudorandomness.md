# 05 — Structure/pseudorandomness

## Claim status: OPEN

No quantitative dichotomy is currently proved. In particular, ordinary
Balog–Szemerédi–Gowers/Freiman output would not by itself preserve the
2-adic information in (1) of `03_2adic_parity.md`.

The desired theorem must specify:

1. a numerical pseudorandomness parameter;
2. a direct bound on the signed dual mass in the pseudorandom branch;
3. a large subset in a bounded-rank progression in the structured branch;
4. an additional 2-adic condition strong enough for the LRC conclusion.

Until those four items are proved with parameter dependencies, both branches
are **OPEN**.
